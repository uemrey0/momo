## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Procedural character engine (`MomoFace`) with springs, twelve moods, idle behaviours,
  cursor attention, poke physics, sleep and wake, event reactions and particles.
- SwiftUI `Canvas` renderer that fits under a hardware notch or hangs from the top centre.
- Menu bar app that shows Momo in the notch, with controls for mood, brain colour, idle life
  and simulated reactions.
- English and Turkish localizations.
- Build tooling (`Makefile`, `Scripts/build-app.sh`), CI, and contribution guidelines.
