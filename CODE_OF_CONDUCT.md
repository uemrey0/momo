# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Momo to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic behaviour
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, chat and
events, and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/uemrey0/momo/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled
confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour they consider harmful. Depending on severity, responses range from a
private warning to a permanent ban.
